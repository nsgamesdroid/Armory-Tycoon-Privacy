Privacy Policy — Armory Tycoon

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Armory Tycoon (the "App") handles the information of its users, on both Android (Google Play) and iOS (App Store). By downloading and using the App, you accept the terms described in this document.

1. Information we collect

The App itself does not collect or store personal data about its users. All game progress is saved locally on the user's device using the app's local storage.

However, the App uses third-party services that may automatically collect certain information:

  • Google AdMob: to show advertisements, both rewarded ads (which the player chooses to watch in exchange for in-game benefits) and interstitial ads. AdMob may collect data such as the device's advertising identifier, IP address and usage data in order to show and measure ads and, if the user consents, to personalize them.
  • Firebase Analytics (Google): to understand how the game is used and to improve it. It records anonymous usage events, such as tutorial steps completed, progress milestones (for example, the first contract or a prestige), decisions made in game events and ads watched. It also receives technical information about the device (model, system version, language, approximate country) and an identifier for the installation of the App. It does not collect your name, email address or precise location, and the App has no user accounts. More information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • Google Play Services (Android): for the App to work on Android devices, including the in-game request to rate the app (Google Play In-App Review).
  • Apple (iOS): the in-game rating request uses the native App Store rating system.
  • GitHub Pages: when the user redeems a promotional code, the App downloads the list of valid codes from GitHub Pages in order to check it. The code you enter is not sent: the check is done on the device itself. As with any Internet connection, GitHub receives the IP address and technical data of the request. You can read its privacy policy at https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

2. Use of information

The information collected by third-party services is used exclusively to:

  • Show relevant ads within the App.
  • Measure ad performance.
  • Improve the user experience and the balance of the game based on aggregated usage statistics.

We never sell or share personal data with third parties other than the services described in this policy.

3. Ads and advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads managed by Google AdMob. AdMob may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your previous visits to this or other apps.

Consent (European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the first time you open the App you will be shown a consent form managed by Google (an IAB-certified consent management platform, through Google User Messaging Platform). In it you can accept or refuse the use of your data for personalized ads. If you do not give your consent, ads will still be shown, but they will not be personalized.

You can change your decision at any time from the "Privacy and ads" option on the game's Settings screen.

You can read Google's privacy policy at:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can opt out of personalized ads by visiting:
https://www.google.com/settings/ads

You can also turn off ad personalization from your device settings: on Android, in Settings → Google → Ads; on iOS, in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics)

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Firebase Analytics usage statistics are only turned on if you give your consent in the form described above. If you refuse, no event is sent. You can change your decision at any time from the "Privacy and ads" option in Settings. In all other regions, statistics are turned on.

Tracking on iOS (App Tracking Transparency)

On iPhone, before showing ads, the App asks for your permission through Apple's system prompt to access the device's advertising identifier (IDFA). If you do not allow it, the App works the same and ads are still shown, but that identifier is not used to track you across apps. You can change your decision at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

4. Data stored locally

The App saves game progress (money, reputation, contracts, completed research, etc.) locally on the device. The content of your game is not sent to external servers and stays on the device. The only thing transmitted is the anonymous Firebase Analytics usage events described in section 1, which do not include your saved game.

The user can delete this data at any time by clearing the app's data from the device settings, or by using the reset option available inside the game.

The App also lets you export and import your progress as a file from the settings menu. This export only happens when the user requests it, and the file is saved or shared only wherever the user chooses.

5. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

6. App permissions

On Android, the App requests only the following permissions:

  • INTERNET: needed to load AdMob ads and to check promotional codes.
  • AD_ID (advertising identifier): used by Google AdMob to show and measure ads, and to personalize them only if the user has consented. You can reset or delete it from your Android device settings.

On iOS, the only permission requested is tracking (App Tracking Transparency), described in section 3.

No permissions are requested to access the camera, microphone, contacts, location or any other sensitive data on the device.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any change by posting the new policy on this page with a new update date. We recommend that you review this page periodically.
